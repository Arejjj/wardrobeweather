# Idea: Wardrobe Templates & Community Sharing

## Core Concept

Users can download pre-built wardrobes created by other users or curated by the app — tailored to specific cities, climates, or regions — so they don't have to fill in their wardrobe from scratch.

## Problem

Building up a wardrobe in the app from zero is time-consuming. New users in particular face a high entry barrier, which may cause them to drop off before they see the app's value.

## Proposal

- A library of **ready-made wardrobe templates** is available in the app
- Templates are organized by:
  - **Region / Climate** (e.g. Tropics, Northern Europe, Mediterranean, Desert, …)
  - **City** (e.g. Hamburg, Bangkok, New York, …)
  - **Season** (e.g. Scandinavian Winter, Southeast Asian Rainy Season, …)
- Users can **download a template** as a starting point and customize it from there
- Users can also **share their own wardrobe** with the community (opt-in)

## Benefits

- Dramatically lowers onboarding friction
- Especially helpful for people who have recently moved to a new climate zone
- Creates a community layer and a sense of shared knowledge
- Templates can also serve as style inspiration

## Technical / Product Notes

- Templates could be curated by the team initially, then expanded via user contributions
- A rating or upvote system would help surface the best templates per region
- Privacy: sharing should always be opt-in; shared wardrobes could be anonymized
- Templates could be versioned (e.g. "Summer 2025 – Berlin")
