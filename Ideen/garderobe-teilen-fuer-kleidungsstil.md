# Idea: Wardrobe Sharing by Style & Fashion Aesthetic

## Core Concept

Beyond weather-based wardrobe templates, users should also be able to share and download wardrobes based on **fashion style and overall aesthetic** — so people can adopt a complete look, not just a climate-appropriate one.

## Extension of the Template Idea

The existing idea of sharing pre-built wardrobes (see: garderobe-vorlagen-und-community-austausch.md) should be broadened: climate is only one dimension. Personal style is equally — or even more — important for many users.

## Style Categories (Examples)

- **Casual / Everyday**
- **Streetwear**
- **Minimalist**
- **Business / Smart Casual**
- **Vintage / Retro**
- **Bohemian**
- **Athleisure / Sporty**
- **Formal / Elegant**
- **Y2K, Cottagecore, Dark Academia, …** (trend-based aesthetics)

## Proposal

- Users can tag their shared wardrobe with one or more style labels
- A **Style Browse** section lets new users explore wardrobes by aesthetic
- Could serve as inspiration even without downloading — a kind of lookbook inside the app
- Combination filters: e.g. "Minimalist + Northern Europe" or "Streetwear + Summer"

## Benefits

- Turns the app into a fashion discovery tool, not just a utility
- Attracts style-conscious users who want more than just weather matching
- Community-driven content keeps the library fresh and trend-aware
- Opens the door for influencer or creator integrations down the line
